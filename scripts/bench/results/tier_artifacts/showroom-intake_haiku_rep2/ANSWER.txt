I wrote a draft `BRIEF.md` in the working directory, but Gate G1 is not passed. The client brief is too thin to build from, so I'm handing back to the `showroom` coordinator with the batched questions below.

## Batched question set for the client

**Product and scope**
1. What are the Lumen's key features and specs (light output, colour temperature, dimming, connectivity such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Matter, or voice assistant support, app)?
2. What pages or sections beyond the hero video and product gallery do you want (features, specs, about, FAQ, contact, press, where to buy)?
3. Is this a single-page site or a multi-page site?
4. Does the site need to sell directly (cart, checkout, payments), link out to a retailer, or only showcase the product?

**Brand and design**
5. Do you have brand guidelines: logo, colour palette, typography, or an existing style guide?
6. Can you give 2–3 reference sites whose feel you like, and any you want to avoid?
7. Should the look be light or dark, and is there a preferred accent colour?

**Assets**
8. Do you have the hero video (file, length, orientation, resolution) and the gallery photos or renders? If not, who will produce them?
9. Do you have product copy, or should we write it? What is the tone of voice?

**Languages, audience and legal**
10. Which languages are needed at launch?
11. Who is the target customer?
12. Do you need a privacy policy, cookie consent, or other legal text? Are there any claims (energy, safety, certifications) that must be approved before publishing?

**Technical and delivery**
13. Do you have a domain, hosting, or preferred tech stack, or should we choose?
14. Are there analytics, email signup, or other third-party integrations to include?
15. What is the target launch date, and who approves the final version?

## Assumptions to accept or reject at G1
- Single-page marketing site, not an e-commerce store.
- Client supplies the hero video and gallery assets.
- English-language copy.
- Responsive web only, no native app.

Once the client answers these, or accepts the assumptions, the brief can be finalized and G1 can pass.
