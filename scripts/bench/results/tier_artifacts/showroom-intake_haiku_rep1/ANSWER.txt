**Assets**
5. Do you have the hero video? If so, its length, orientation, and resolution. If not, who produces it?
6. How many gallery images do you have, and do you have high-resolution originals?
7. Do you have a logo, brand guidelines, colour palette, and typefaces?
8. Do you have product photography, lifestyle imagery, or only renders?

**Content**
9. Who writes the copy: you, or should we draft it? Do you have approved product names, headlines, and feature descriptions?
10. Do you need specifications (dimensions, bulb, connectivity, app compatibility, warranty)? If so, supply them.
11. Are there legal requirements: privacy policy, cookie consent, regulatory claims, or accessibility standard (e.g., WCAG 2.1 AA)?

**Design direction**
12. Can you give examples of sites whose feel you want (“calm and premium”)? Include ones you want to avoid.
13. Colour direction: light or dark theme, and any palette preferences?
14. Should the hero video autoplay muted and loop, or should it be user-initiated? Is a poster fallback needed?

**Technical and delivery**
15. Is there an existing domain, hosting, or CMS, or should we choose one?
16. Are there analytics, tag manager, or consent tools to integrate?
17. Are there performance targets (e.g., page weight limit, Lighthouse score) given the video load?
18. What browsers and devices must be supported?
19. Is there a target launch date and a point of contact for approvals?
