# BRIEF.md — Lumen Showcase Website

**Status:** DRAFT. Blocked at Gate G1 until the open questions below are answered.

## Known (from client brief)
- Product: Lumen, a smart lamp.
- Deliverable: a showcase website.
- Tone: calm, premium.
- Required sections: hero video, product gallery.

## Assumptions (unconfirmed, to be overridden by answers)
- Single-page, responsive, static site.
- No e-commerce or checkout. CTA links out to an existing store, if any.
- English only.

## Open questions (batched)
**Goal and audience**
1. What is the primary goal: drive purchases, collect leads or waitlist sign-ups, or build brand awareness?
2. Who is the target audience (consumers, interior designers, B2B)?
3. What should the main call to action be, and where does it point (store URL, email form, none)?

**Content and assets**
4. Do you have a hero video? If not, who produces it? Give its length, format and whether it has audio.
5. How many gallery images are there? Are they final, and what resolution and licensing do they have? Are there lifestyle shots as well as product shots?
6. Do you have copy: tagline, product description, feature list, specs, pricing? Or should we draft it?
7. What smart features must be shown (app, voice assistant, Matter/HomeKit, dimming, color temperature)?

**Brand and design**
8. Do you have a logo, brand colors, fonts, or a style guide?
9. Name 2–3 reference sites you like (and any you dislike).
10. Is dark or light theme preferred? Should the site include motion and animation, such as parallax?

**Scope and technical**
11. Which pages or sections beyond hero and gallery are wanted (features, specs, FAQ, reviews, contact)?
12. Languages needed?
13. Do you have a domain and hosting, or a preferred stack/CMS?
14. Is analytics or cookie consent required? Any accessibility or legal requirements (WCAG level, GDPR)?

**Constraints**
15. Deadline or launch date? Budget range?
16. Who approves deliverables, and what is the review process?
