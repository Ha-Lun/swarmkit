## Intake summary

The client wants a showcase website for "Lumen", a smart lamp. The feel is calm and premium, with a hero video and a product gallery. The client said "That's all I have for now."

## Batched question set (for the client)

**Product**
1. What are Lumen's key features and specs (light output, colour temperature, control method, connectivity, dimensions, materials)?
2. What price, availability, and launch date should the site show, if any?
3. Are there claims that need legal or certification backing (energy, safety, warranty, sustainability)?

**Brand and style**
4. Do you have a logo, colour palette, typography, or a style guide? If not, should we propose one?
5. Name two or three premium websites or brands whose feel you like, and one you don't want it to resemble.

**Media**
6. Does the hero video exist? If so, what format, length, resolution, and who owns the licence? If not, who produces it?
7. How many gallery images or clips exist, at what resolution, and with what licensing?

**Scope and content**
8. How many pages do you want? Options: a single landing page, or multiple pages (about, specs, FAQ, contact).
9. Which sections are required beyond the hero and gallery (features, specs, reviews, press, story)?
10. What is the call to action: buy link, retailer links, waitlist/email signup, enquiry form, or none?
11. Is there any e-commerce, checkout, or account functionality? (Assumed: none.)

**Audience and markets**
12. Who is the target customer, and in which countries?
13. Which languages are needed, and is there a right-to-left requirement?

**Technical and compliance**
14. Do you have a hosting provider, domain name, or technology preference (static site, specific framework, CMS)?
15. What accessibility target applies (e.g. WCAG 2.2 AA)? Are there performance targets (page-load budget, mobile-first)?
16. What privacy requirements apply? Will you use analytics, cookies, or a newsletter tool that needs consent handling?

**Delivery**
17. What is the launch deadline, and is there a budget ceiling that affects scope?
18. Who is the point of contact, and who approves the final output?
