## Batched questions for the client

**Goal and audience**
1. What should the site achieve: drive pre-orders or purchases, generate leads or retailer enquiries, or build brand awareness?
2. What is the single main call to action (for example "Buy now", "Join the waitlist" or "Find a retailer")? Where should it lead?
3. Who is the target audience (consumers, interior designers, B2B)? Which markets?

**Content and assets**
4. Does the hero video already exist? If so, send the length, format and who owns the rights. If not, should we plan for a stock or placeholder video, or does production need to be scoped separately?
5. How many gallery images are there, and are they product shots, lifestyle shots or both? Do you have the rights to use them? Are there any 3D renders?
6. What are the key product facts: features, smart-home compatibility (app, voice assistants, protocols), specs, colours or variants, and price?
7. Who writes the copy: you or us? Is there a tagline?

**Brand and design**
8. Is there a brand kit (logo, colours, fonts, tone of voice)?
9. Can you give 2–3 reference sites you like, and 1–2 you dislike?
10. Is "calm and premium" meant as dark and moody, light and airy, or something else? Should the hero video autoplay with sound, or stay silent?

**Scope and technical**
11. Should it be a single scrolling page or multiple pages (for example specs, FAQ, contact)? Do you need a store or checkout, or only links out?
12. Which languages are needed?
13. Do you have a domain and hosting preference, or an existing CMS or stack we must use?
14. Do you need analytics, a newsletter signup or a cookie-consent banner?

**Logistics**
15. What are the deadline and budget range?
16. Who is the single approver for sign-off at each gate?

## Assumptions in the draft
These are not binding until confirmed:
- A single-page site is acceptable.
- The site is a static front end with no checkout.
