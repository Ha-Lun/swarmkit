# BRIEF: Lumen Smart Lamp Showcase Website

Status: DRAFT, INCOMPLETE. Gate G1 not passed. Open questions listed in Section 5.

## 1. Summary

Build a showcase website for Lumen, a smart lamp sold by the client.

## 2. Confirmed requirements

- Product: Lumen, a smart lamp.
- Deliverable type: showcase (marketing) website.
- Tone: calm and premium.
- Required sections:
  - Hero video (full-width, above the fold).
  - Product gallery.

## 3. Assumptions (unconfirmed, to be validated)

- A.1 Single-page or small multi-page marketing site; no e-commerce checkout.
- A.2 Responsive web design (desktop and mobile).
- A.3 English-language copy.
- A.4 Client supplies all video, image, and copy assets.

## 4. Out of scope (provisional)

- Checkout, cart, and payments.
- User accounts.
- Lamp companion app or device firmware.

## 5. Open questions (batched, to be answered by the client)

**Scope and goals**
1. What is the primary goal of the site: brand awareness, pre-orders, sales, email sign-ups, or something else?
2. Which pages do you need (for example: home, product detail, about, contact, press, FAQ)? Is any e-commerce or checkout needed?
3. Is there a launch date or deadline?

**Audience and messaging**
4. Who is the target customer, and in which markets?
5. What are the three to five key selling points of Lumen (for example: light quality, smart features, design, materials, price tier)?
6. What is the price, and should price be displayed on the site?
7. Are there competitors whose sites you admire or want to avoid?

**Brand and design**
8. Do you have brand guidelines: logo, color palette, typography, and existing tone-of-voice notes?
9. Can you describe "calm and premium" with examples (reference sites, colors, imagery style, motion level)?
10. Should the site be light, dark, or both?

**Content assets**
11. Do you have the hero video? If yes, its length, resolution, orientation, and whether it has audio. If not, who produces it?
12. How many product photos are available for the gallery, and do they include lifestyle shots, detail shots, and packshots on white?
13. Is product copy (headlines, descriptions, specs) written, or must we write it?
14. Are there product specs to display (dimensions, weight, light output in lumens, color temperature, connectivity, power, warranty)?
15. Are there legal or compliance texts needed (privacy policy, terms, cookie consent, safety certifications, CE/FCC marks)?

**Technical and delivery**
16. Is there an existing domain, hosting, or CMS? Or should we propose one?
17. Which languages are required, and is localization needed?
18. Are there analytics, tracking, or marketing tools to integrate (e.g. GA, Meta Pixel, newsletter provider)?
19. Any accessibility target (for example WCAG 2.1 AA)? Must the video have captions?
20. Performance constraints: maximum page weight, target devices and browsers, or a need for a video fallback on mobile?

**Ownership and process**
21. Who is the single point of contact for approvals, and how many review rounds do you expect?
22. Who owns final copy and imagery rights?
