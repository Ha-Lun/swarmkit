## Batched question set for the client

**Scope and goals**
1. What is the primary goal of the site: brand awareness, pre-orders, sales, email sign-ups, or something else?
2. Which pages do you need (for example: home, product detail, about, contact, press, FAQ)? Is any e-commerce or checkout needed?
3. Is there a launch date or deadline?

**Audience and messaging**
4. Who is the target customer, and in which markets?
5. What are the three to five key selling points of Lumen (for example: light quality, smart features, design, materials, price tier)?
6. What is the price, and should price be displayed on the site?
7. Are there competitors whose sites you admire or want to avoid?

**Brand and design**
8. Do you have brand guidelines: logo, colour palette, typography, and tone-of-voice notes?
9. Can you describe "calm and premium" with examples (reference sites, colours, imagery style, motion level)?
10. Should the site be light, dark, or both?

**Content assets**
11. Do you have the hero video? If so, what are its length, resolution, orientation, and does it have audio? If not, who produces it?
12. How many product photos are available for the gallery, and do they include lifestyle shots, detail shots, and packshots on white?
13. Is product copy (headlines, descriptions, specs) written, or must we write it?
14. Which product specs should be displayed (dimensions, weight, light output in lumens, colour temperature, connectivity, power, warranty)?
15. Which legal or compliance texts are needed (privacy policy, terms, cookie consent, safety certifications, CE/FCC marks)?

**Technical and delivery**
16. Is there an existing domain, hosting, or CMS, or should we propose one?
17. Which languages are required, and is localisation needed?
18. Which analytics, tracking, or marketing tools should be integrated (for example GA, Meta Pixel, a newsletter provider)?
19. Is there an accessibility target (for example WCAG 2.1 AA)? Must the video have captions?
20. Are there performance constraints: maximum page weight, target devices and browsers, or a need for a mobile video fallback?

**Ownership and process**
21. Who is the single point of contact for approvals, and how many review rounds do you expect?
22. Who owns the final copy and imagery rights?
