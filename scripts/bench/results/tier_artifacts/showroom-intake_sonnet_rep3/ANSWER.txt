**What the brief already settles:** The product is Lumen, a smart lamp. The deliverable is a showcase website with a calm, premium tone, a hero video and a product gallery.

**Questions for the client**
1. **Goal and audience**
   - Q1. What is the main goal of the site: drive purchases, collect leads or pre-orders, or build brand awareness?
   - Q2. Who is the target buyer (for example home users, interior designers, or B2B/hospitality)? Which markets and languages?
2. **Content and assets**
   - Q3. Does the hero video already exist? If so, send the file or link, its length and its resolution. If not, who will produce it?
   - Q4. How many gallery images are there, and are they professional product or lifestyle shots? Are there any 3D renders?
   - Q5. Who writes the copy (headline, features, specs), and is it ready? What are the 3–5 key selling points of Lumen?
3. **Scope**
   - Q6. Is this a single page or several pages? Should it include sections such as features, specs, FAQ, reviews or contact?
   - Q7. What is the primary call to action? Examples are "Buy now" linking to an existing shop, a pre-order form, or "Contact sales".
   - Q8. Do you need e-commerce or checkout on this site, or only links out? Any integrations such as analytics, newsletter, or CRM?
4. **Brand and design**
   - Q9. Do you have a logo, a color palette, fonts, or brand guidelines? Please share 2–3 reference sites you like and one you dislike.
   - Q10. Any accessibility or motion constraints? For example, should the video autoplay muted, or should reduced-motion users get a still image?
5. **Technical and delivery**
   - Q11. Do you have a domain and hosting preference, or an existing CMS or stack that must be used? Should the client be able to edit content afterwards?
   - Q12. Are there legal requirements, such as cookie consent or GDPR, or required legal pages?
   - Q13. What is the deadline or launch date, and what is the budget range?
   - Q14. Who is the single approver on the client side?

**Working assumptions in the draft, to be replaced by answers:**
- The site is a static, single-page, responsive marketing site.
- The hero video autoplays muted, with a poster fallback.
- The gallery is a lightbox-style image grid.
- There is no checkout, so the call to action links out.
