The client's brief only gives the product (Lumen, a smart lamp), a calm and premium tone, and the two required pieces: a hero video and a product gallery.

## Batched questions for the client
If a question has no answer, the default in brackets applies.

**Goals and audience**
1. What should the site achieve: brand awareness, pre-orders, driving traffic to a retailer, or investor and press interest? What is the primary call to action?
2. Who is the target audience (consumers, interior designers, B2B)? Which markets and languages? [English only]
3. Is this a showcase only, or does it need a buy button or checkout? [Showcase only, with a link out]

**Scope and content**
4. Which sections beyond the hero video and gallery do you want (features, specs, app and smart-home integrations, reviews, FAQ, contact or newsletter signup)? [Single page: hero, features, gallery, specs, contact]
5. Who writes the copy: you or us? Do you have a tagline and key product claims?
6. Which smart features and specs must be shown (for example connectivity, voice assistants, app, color temperature)?

**Assets**
7. Does the hero video already exist? If so, what are its length, format and resolution? If not, who produces it? Should it autoplay silently on loop?
8. How many gallery images are there, and are they product shots, lifestyle shots, or both? Do you have a 3D model or an interactive viewer? What resolution and licensing do they have?
9. Do you have a logo, brand guidelines, colors and fonts?

**Look and feel**
10. Please give 2–3 reference sites or brands you like, and 1–2 you dislike. What does "calm and premium" mean to you (muted palette, dark or light, lots of whitespace)?
11. Are there accessibility requirements (WCAG level, reduced-motion support)? [WCAG AA]

**Technical and delivery**
12. Do you have a domain and hosting, or a preferred stack or CMS? Do you need to edit content yourself later? [Static site, no CMS]
13. Do you need analytics, cookie consent or other compliance (GDPR)? [Basic privacy-friendly analytics]
14. What are the deadline and budget range? Is there a launch event or date?
15. Who approves the work, and in how many review rounds?
