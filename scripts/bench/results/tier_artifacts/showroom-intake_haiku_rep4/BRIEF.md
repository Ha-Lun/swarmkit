## Batched question set

Please answer all of the following in one reply.

1. **Hero video**: Does the video exist already? If so, provide the file(s), length, and orientation. If not, who produces it? Should it autoplay muted and loop, or be user-controlled? Is there a poster image for loading and reduced-motion fallback?
2. **Product gallery**: Do product photos exist? How many, and what resolution? Should the gallery be a grid, a carousel, or a lightbox? Are there detail or lifestyle shots, and do you need captions?
3. **Product facts**: What are Lumen's key specs (dimensions, weight, light output, colour temperature range, power, connectivity such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Matter, or Zigbee)? Which app or voice assistants does it support? Price and currency, if it should be shown?
4. **Copy**: Who writes the copy: you, us, or a copywriter? Do you have existing taglines, product descriptions, or brand guidelines? What languages are needed, and is the site English only?
5. **Brand assets**: Do you have a logo, colour palette, typefaces, and a brand style guide? Any existing brand site to match or avoid?
6. **Call to action and scope**: What should visitors do? Options include buy (linking to an external shop), pre-order, sign up for a newsletter, find a retailer, or contact sales. Is any e-commerce or checkout needed? Which pages beyond the single landing page are required (e.g. specs, about, support, FAQ, legal)?
7. **Calm, premium feel: references**: Name two or three websites you like the look of, and one you dislike. Any preferred palette (e.g. warm neutrals, monochrome), motion level, or typography style?
8. **Accessibility and performance targets**: Is WCAG 2.1 AA the required standard? Any target for page weight or load time on mobile? Must the site work without JavaScript for the hero?
9. **Hosting and domain**: Is there a domain and hosting already? Any preferred stack or platform (static site, a CMS, or an existing framework), or should we choose?
10. **Analytics, cookies, and consent**: Do you need analytics or marketing tags? If so, which, and does the site need a cookie consent banner for your target markets (e.g. EU/Sweden)?
11. **Legal and compliance**: Any required legal pages (privacy policy, terms, imprint, warranty, or product safety/CE/electrical certifications to display)? Are there regulated claims (energy, health, or eye-comfort claims) that need approval wording?
12. **Timeline and approval**: Target launch date? Who signs off on designs and copy, and how many review rounds do you expect?
13. **Markets and SEO**: Which countries or regions will the site target? Are there any SEO keywords or metadata you want?
14. **Deliverables and handover**: Do you need source code, a deployable build, a content editing workflow for non-developers, or only a hosted site?

## Assumptions (until the client says otherwise)

- Single-page showcase with a hero video section and a product gallery, plus a single external call-to-action.
- English only, responsive for desktop and mobile.
- No checkout or customer accounts.
- Client supplies all media and copy.
